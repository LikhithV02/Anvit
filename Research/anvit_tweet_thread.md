# Anvit Tweet Thread

**Tweet 1 (Hook)**
> I built an app that lets you chat with your PDFs and Word docs — completely offline, no cloud, no subscriptions, nothing leaves your phone. It's called **Anvit** and it just went live on the Play Store 🧵
>
> 📲 https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.likhith.anvit

---

**Tweet 2 (The problem it solves)**
> We all have that pile of PDFs. Research papers, contracts, lecture notes, reports. You know the answer is *in there somewhere* but finding it means rereading the whole thing. That's the problem Anvit fixes.

---

**Tweet 3 (Core feature)**
> You just ask a question. Anvit finds the exact passages from your documents and gives you a grounded answer with **cited sources** — so you always know where the info came from. No hallucinations, no guessing.

---

**Tweet 4 (The offline + privacy angle)**
> The whole thing runs on-device using **Gemma 4 (2B or 4B)** via Google LiteRT. Once you download the model, it's 100% offline. Your documents, questions, and conversations never touch a server. Not mine. Not anyone's. 🔒

---

**Tweet 5 (Agentic RAG — the technical magic, made simple)**
> Under the hood it uses something called **Agentic RAG** — a fancy way of saying: instead of doing one dumb keyword search, the app breaks your question into focused steps, retrieves info across multiple passes, and refines the answer before showing it to you.

📎 _Attach image: `agentic_flow_diagram.png`_

---

**Tweet 6 (Corrective RAG)**
> There's also a **self-correction layer**. If the first retrieval is weak or off-target, it automatically rephrases and retries instead of making stuff up. It knows when it doesn't know. That's rare.

---

**Tweet 7 (Hybrid search)**
> Search uses both **semantic** (meaning-based) and **BM25** (keyword-based) retrieval at the same time. Basically — it finds what you *mean*, not just the exact words you typed.
