## Short Description

Private, on-device AI for your documents. Chat with PDFs. No cloud. No tracking.

## Long Description

Anvit is a private, on-device AI assistant that helps you chat with PDFs, uncover key insights, compare documents, and get clear answers grounded in your own files. Whether you're reviewing research, study material, reports, manuals, or notes, Anvit helps you find what matters faster without sending your data away.

Built with advanced agentic RAG, Anvit goes beyond simple document search. It can break complex questions into smaller steps, retrieve the most relevant passages, refine weak results, and generate sharper answers from your document library.

No cloud. No tracking. No data collection. Your documents, questions, and conversations never leave your phone.

### Key Features

- **100% On-Device AI** — Gemma 4 (2B or 4B) runs locally through Google LiteRT. Once models are downloaded, the app works fully offline.
- **Agentic RAG Pipeline** — Complex questions are automatically broken into focused sub-queries, retrieved across multiple passes, and refined before you see the answer.
- **Hybrid Retrieval** — Combines dense semantic search with BM25 keyword search for the best of both worlds. Finds what you mean, not just what you type.
- **Corrective RAG (CRAG)** — When retrieval quality is poor, the app automatically re-queries with rephrased terms instead of hallucinating.
- **Multiple Document Collections** — Organize your PDFs into named collections and query each independently.
- **Cited Sources** — Every answer includes the source document and the exact passages used.

### Great For

- Students studying textbooks and research papers
- Professionals reviewing contracts, reports, and specifications
- Researchers querying large PDF archives
- Anyone who values privacy over convenience

Anvit is built as an open demonstration that powerful AI no longer requires sending your data to someone else's servers. Your phone is enough.
